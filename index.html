A CLEAR METHOD

Careful testing.

The goal is not a long list of scanner output. It is a reliable assessment that helps your team make better security decisions.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before we get started.

Do you test production systems?

Staging is preferred. Production testing is considered only when explicitly authorized, risk-assessed and covered by written rules of engagement.

Do you need credentials?

For authenticated testing, client-provided test accounts with agreed roles are usually needed. Real customer credentials should not be shared.

Will this make us compliant?

No. An application security assessment is not a certification or guarantee of SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS or any other compliance status.

How are findings confirmed?

Potential issues are reviewed for reproducibility, scope and impact before being described as validated findings. Limitations are documented.

Can you work with a development agency?

Referral or subcontracting arrangements can be discussed. The client, authorization, responsibilities and reporting path must be clear.

What happens before testing begins?

We agree target assets, authorization, scope, exclusions, schedule, emergency contact, data handling and stop conditions in writing.
